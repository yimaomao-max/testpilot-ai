# T0.4 付费意愿承诺(LOI)模板

> 目的:在开发 Phase 2 之前拿到 2–3 份书面付费承诺,验证"小团队愿不愿意每月付 $19"。
> 使用时机:访谈第 5 节回答"立即/试用"且态度积极时,当场或 24 小时内发送。

## 英文版(主用,海外市场)

---

**Letter of Intent — Early Access Pricing**

Date: ____________

I confirm that [Company / Team name] is interested in using **Spec2Test AI**, a tool that converts product requirements into reviewable, executable Playwright tests.

Based on the demo and our current testing workflow, we expect the product to replace approximately ____ hours/month of manual test design and failure investigation for our team.

If the product delivers the following at launch, we intend to subscribe:

- [ ] Import requirements/user stories and generate reviewable test cases linked to acceptance criteria
- [ ] Export standard Playwright TypeScript tests we own and can run in our own CI
- [ ] Failure reports that classify the cause (app bug / locator change / script error / environment) with evidence

**Committed plan:** ☐ Starter ($9/mo) ☐ Pro ($19/mo) ☐ Team ($49/mo)
**Expected start:** within ____ weeks of general availability.
**Team seats needed:** ____

Name / Role: ____________________
Email: ____________________

*Non-binding letter of intent for early-access planning. No payment is due until the product is available and meets the above scope.*

---

## 中文版(备用)

**意向书 — 早期定价承诺**

我确认 [团队名] 有意使用 Spec2Test AI(将产品需求转化为可审核、可执行的 Playwright 测试)。

若产品上线时满足以下范围,我们意向订阅:
- [ ] 导入需求并生成关联验收标准的可审核用例
- [ ] 导出标准 Playwright TypeScript 测试(代码归我们,可在自有 CI 运行)
- [ ] 失败报告可分类原因(产品缺陷/元素变化/脚本错误/环境)并附证据

意向套餐:☐ Starter($9/月)☐ Pro($19/月)☐ Team($49/月)
预计开通时间:GA 后 ____ 周内;席位:____

*非约束性意向,产品可用且满足上述范围前无需付款。*

## 跟进节奏

| 时间点 | 动作 |
|---|---|
| 发送后 3 天 | 未回复则跟进一次(附演示视频链接) |
| 达成 M2/M3 里程碑 | 向 LOI 持有人发送进度更新(培养期待) |
| 产品可注册时 | 给 LOI 持有人 14 天免费 Pro + 创始会员折扣(锁定首批付费) |

## 判据

- ≥2 份签署 LOI(含具体套餐与席位数)→ 通过,进入 Phase 1 开发
- 0 份但 ≥5 份口头积极 → 降低范围做单功能 MVP(只做失败分析或只做用例生成),再验证
